    How to play

    Reach 10 victory points to win. Place two settlements and two roads during setup, then roll dice to collect resources and expand.

    • Settlements score 1 point. Cities score 2 and produce twice as much.
    • Leave at least one empty intersection between settlements and cities.
    • On a 7, players holding more than 7 resources discard half. The active player then moves the robber.
    • Trade with the bank or use ports for better rates.
    • New development cards cannot be played until your next turn. Play at most one per turn.
    • Longest Road and Largest Army are worth 2 points each.

    Select a build item, select a highlighted location, then confirm. All building costs and bank trades are shown on the main screen.

    This version supports bank and port trades. One human plays against 1–3 bots.
